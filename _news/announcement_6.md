Four papers accepted at **ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2026)**: **Internalizing Investment Mandates: A Neurosymbolic RL Approach to Portfolio Allocation**, by Zeynep Yildiz, Satchit Chatterji, Ercan Atam, and Erman Acar; **From Communities to Trades: Network-Guided Pair Selection and Adaptive Thresholding for Cryptocurrency Statistical Arbitrage**, by Alexandre du Prez, Mehul Verma, and Erman Acar; **Amortized Interventional Forecasting for Multivariate CIR Processes**, by Andreas Sauter, Sumit Shourab, Drona Kandhai, and Erman Acar; and **From Features to Circuits: Mechanistic Interpretability for Financial Tabular Transformers**, by Arco van Breda, Saba Amiri, Ana Oprescu, Sander Klous, and Erman Acar.
